# Research Unit

ID: R010
Title: Lebesgue — measure, integration, and functions beyond Riemann's boundary
Period: approximately 1870–1910

## Purpose

Return to analysis after R007–R009 have broadened the Atlas. Follow a precise internal mathematical problem: limitations of Riemann-style integration and the need to control sets on which functions behave badly.

## Central Atlas Question

**How should integration change when discontinuities and exceptional sets become too complicated for the older framework?**

## Historical scope

Investigate:

1. Which limitations of Riemann integration were salient before and for Lebesgue.
2. The roles of Jordan content/measure, Borel sets/measure, and other intermediaries.
3. What Lebesgue's early definition of measure and integral actually says.
4. How the order of operations changes conceptually from partitioning the domain to grouping values/ranges, while avoiding oversimplified textbook slogans.
5. How convergence questions enter Lebesgue's work and what later convergence theorems can or cannot be attributed to the earliest phase.
6. To what extent Cantor/point-set developments are a historical prerequisite versus a modern structural relation.

## Origin pattern

Primarily **internal mathematical development**, with likely **cross-field recombination** between analysis and emerging point-set/set-measure methods.

Any R008 → R010 transition must be supported by actual historical evidence, not by the modern fact that measure theory uses sets.

## Integration target

Strong expected intersections:

- R004 Riemann integrability;
- R008 exceptional/point-set questions if historical transmission is supported.

Possible downstream paths:

- convergence theorems;
- probability/measure;
- functional analysis;
- Hilbert-space methods.

## Historical risks to test

- "Lebesgue invented measure theory" without Jordan, Borel, and other intermediaries.
- Presenting Lebesgue integration merely as "Riemann but better".
- Projecting modern sigma-algebra language into the earliest sources without qualification.
- Assuming Cantor → Borel → Lebesgue as a clean causal chain.
- Importing later dominated convergence / Lp-space theory into the initial development.

## Expected Story direction

Candidate Story:

**How much bad behavior can integration tolerate?**

Possible arc:

Riemann boundary → increasingly complicated exceptional sets → measure of sets → new integral → broader convergence/integration questions.

## Boundaries

Do not absorb:

- full probability theory;
- abstract measure spaces in modern generality;
- Lp functional analysis;
- Radon–Nikodym theory;
- twentieth-century integration theory beyond what is required for immediate historical consequences.
