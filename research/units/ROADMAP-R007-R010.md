# Work design — R007 through R010

## Goal

After the completed R005 analysis capstone and R006 algebra opening, broaden the Atlas deliberately rather than continuing one field chronologically.

This sequence opens Geometry, creates an Analysis → set-theoretic branch, thickens Algebra through algebraic-number structure, and then returns to Analysis through measure/integration.

## Sequence

### R007 — Gauss / intrinsic curvature

Open the first substantial Geometry cluster.

Purpose:

- establish geometry as a genuine branch rather than a future placeholder;
- center the mathematical Question of intrinsic curvature;
- keep geodesy/surveying as historical context where warranted;
- prepare a later, separately researched route to Riemannian geometry and topology.

Do not connect R007 to R004 merely because both later involve Riemann.

### R008 — Cantor / uniqueness and exceptional point sets

Branch from the Fourier/Dirichlet/Riemann line into a new internal question: uniqueness of trigonometric representation.

Purpose:

- show mathematics generating new Questions internally;
- test the familiar claim that Fourier-series work "led to set theory";
- make exceptional/derived point sets a researched bridge rather than a retrospective shortcut.

This is the first intended route toward Foundations/Set-theoretic questions, but it is not yet a full unit on cardinality or transfinite arithmetic.

### R009 — Dedekind / ideals and factorization

Return to Algebra, but through arithmetic structure rather than solvability.

Purpose:

- prevent the algebra branch from being only a Galois/group genealogy;
- investigate failure of unique factorization, Kummer's ideal numbers, and Dedekind's ideals;
- prepare later work on abstract algebra, Noether, algebraic number theory, and algebraic geometry.

Do not manufacture a direct Galois → Dedekind influence chain.

### R010 — Lebesgue / measure and integration

Return to Analysis only after three units have broadened the forest.

Purpose:

- connect back to R004's integrability boundary;
- investigate Jordan/Borel/Lebesgue intermediaries;
- test whether the emerging point-set line from R008 is historically connected to measure/integration strongly enough for a reviewed cross-field handoff;
- open downstream paths to probability and functional analysis.

## Why this portfolio

The intended forest after R010 is approximately:

```text
Analysis
  Fourier → Cauchy → Dirichlet → Riemann → quantified limits
      │                       │
      │                       └→ uniqueness / exceptional sets → R008
      └──────────────────────────────────────────────→ R010 measure/integration

Algebra
  solvability / permutations / Galois → R006
  algebraic numbers / factorization / ideals → R009

Geometry
  curvature / intrinsic surface geometry → R007
```

The graph should remain willing to show disconnected clusters. A visually connected forest is not worth unsupported historical edges.

## Cross-unit review checkpoints

After each unit:

1. inspect whether the new Story duplicates an existing path;
2. add cross-Story transitions only where independently reviewed evidence supports them;
3. verify Person navigation and field framing;
4. preserve unresolved gaps rather than smoothing them into continuity.

After R010, perform a forest-level review before assigning R011:

- Is Geometry still too thin?
- Does Algebra need abstraction/Noether next?
- Is the Cantor branch ready for cardinality/foundations?
- Should probability now enter through measure?
- Is it time to backfill the early Kepler–Galileo–Newton / mechanics root?
- Is a mathematical-physics/Heaviside branch now structurally useful?
