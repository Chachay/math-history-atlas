# Research Unit

ID: R009
Title: Dedekind — failure of unique factorization, ideals, and algebraic number structure
Period: approximately 1840–1895

## Purpose

Thicken the algebra branch opened by R006 by following a different internal problem: ordinary factorization behavior fails in algebraic number settings, and new objects are introduced to recover a usable structural theory.

## Central Atlas Question

**What should replace ordinary factors when unique factorization fails?**

## Historical scope

Investigate:

1. The factorization problems arising in algebraic number theory before Dedekind.
2. Kummer's ideal numbers and the historical relation to Dedekind's later ideal theory.
3. What Dedekind meant by ideals in the relevant editions/texts and how the formulation changed over time.
4. How unique factorization is recovered at the level of ideals, with care about modern ring-theoretic reformulation.
5. The relation among algebraic numbers, algebraic integers, fields/orders, modules, and ideals in the historical sources.
6. Differences between Dedekind's and Kronecker's approaches where necessary to avoid presenting one path as inevitable.

## Origin pattern

Primarily **internal mathematical development**:

factorization problem → ideal-number/ideal response → structural questions about algebraic number systems.

## Integration target

Primary expected connection:

- R006's polynomial/algebraic-solvability branch, through algebraic numbers and roots, only where historically justified.

The unit should make Algebra visibly broader than Galois solvability: equations are one entry point, arithmetic structure another.

Possible future handoffs:

- abstract ring/ideal theory;
- Noether;
- algebraic number theory;
- algebraic geometry.

## Historical risks to test

- "Dedekind invented ideals" without Kummer and changing editions/context.
- Translating Dedekind directly into modern commutative algebra notation and concepts.
- Claiming a simple R006 Galois → Dedekind influence chain merely from subject proximity.
- Treating unique factorization of ideals as conceptually identical to modern textbook treatment without source-level qualification.
- Ignoring competing Kroneckerian approaches.

## Expected Story direction

Candidate Story:

**What survives when unique factorization fails?**

Possible arc:

algebraic numbers → failed element factorization → ideal numbers / ideals → restored structural factorization → new algebraic objects.

## Boundaries

Do not absorb:

- full construction of the real numbers;
- full field theory history;
- Noetherian rings/modules;
- class field theory;
- algebraic geometry.
